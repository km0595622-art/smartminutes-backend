Information We May Process

When you authorize Smart Minutes AI to interact with a Facebook Page, the application may process information necessary to authenticate the connection and manage the authorized Page.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide the requested social-media management functionality, including connecting an authorized Facebook Page and publishing or managing content according to the permissions granted to the application.

Facebook Information

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the application and restrict access to authorized systems.

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain the connection, comply with legal obligations, or address security and operational issues.
